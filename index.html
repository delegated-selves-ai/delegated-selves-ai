When an agent

01Why this workshop

Delegation changes
the social contract.

AI agents are moving into group chats, workplaces, online communities, and public platforms. They draft and send messages, negotiate, remember, and sometimes present a persistent persona.

These actions can expose information about a user, a relationship, or an entire group. They also blur who consented, who is accountable, and who is speaking.

01Shared vocabulary

A map of privacy and security risks that emerge under delegation.

02Design principles

Patterns for consent, provenance, accountability, and collective control.
